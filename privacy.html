Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

This page explains what personal information Orienting handles when you use this website or contact me.

Information you send

If you email me, I receive your email address and anything you include in your message. I use it to answer your enquiry, assess whether I can help, and manage any work we agree to do.

Website services

This site does not use analytics, advertising trackers, newsletter forms, or marketing cookies.

The site is hosted by Vercel. Like most hosting providers, Vercel may process technical request data such as IP address, browser type, and time of access to deliver and secure the site. Vercel privacy policy ↗

Google Fonts supplies the typefaces used on this site. Google may receive technical request data when your browser loads those files. Google privacy policy ↗

How long information is kept

I keep correspondence only as long as needed for the enquiry, our working relationship, and any legal or accounting duties. You can ask me to delete it when those duties no longer apply.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the use of your personal information. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

To ask about your information, email hi@orienting.lu. I will respond as soon as practical and within the period required by applicable law.
